Fluid at Rest

The vortex spirals inward and elongates like spaghetti. It concentrates, speeding up as it shrinks, but the energy remains finite — the key constraint from physics.

In finite time, the fluid velocity grows without bound — a singularity. Yet kinetic energy remains finite, as the laws of physics demand. The continuum model breaks down.
